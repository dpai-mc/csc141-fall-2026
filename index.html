About this page

This page is a map of the course. It lists every lesson, activity, and guide, in the week it belongs to, so you can find things without digging through Canvas. Links open in a new tab.

Note

Due dates and grades are in Canvas, not here. This page tells you where things are; Canvas tells you when they're due and how you did. If the two ever disagree, Canvas is right.

Start Here

Week 1, in order. Do the first one before anything else.

  1. Get into Gemini. It's free with your college login and it's the tool the class uses as its baseline. Gemini tool guide →
  2. Work through Lesson 1 — tool onboarding, and your "10 Things" baseline list. M1L1: AI Tool Onboarding →
  3. Then Lesson 2 — what AI, machine learning, and deep learning actually mean. M1L2: Introduction to AI Concepts →
Caution

Can't get into Gemini? Use Microsoft Copilot instead — also free with your student login, and every activity works in it. Don't let an access problem stall your first week; tell your instructor and keep going.
