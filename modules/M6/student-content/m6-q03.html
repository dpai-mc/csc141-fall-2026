What You’ll Do

Your last file, then the whole folder.

Read this section

Revisit your week-1 list, write a new one for work, write the synthesis that ties your semester together — then check your folder and hand it all in. What a synthesis is, how to revisit the list and the full file list are in M6L4; this page is where you do it.

The three parts

  • Part 1 — 10 Things, then and now: your week-1 list, item by item kept, changed or removed, with a note on each; plus a new list of things AI can help you do at work.
  • Part 2 — your synthesis: one to two pages connecting your work across the semester, ending with your plan for staying AI-literate. It includes your Module 6 reflection.
  • Part 3 — the folder: all fifteen files checked, zipped and handed in.

Where’s your week-1 list?

In your folder as 01-ten-things-baseline.pdf. If it’s missing, check what you submitted in Canvas in week 1, or contact your instructor through Canvas Inbox.

Note

Due Thursday, December 10 — not Sunday. The semester ends Friday. Canvas is the authority on dates.

Work block 3 — your synthesis

60 minutes Earns: Synthesis + Self-awareness + Forward strategy

Write one to two pages connecting your work across the semester, then your plan for what comes next.

Note

Questions to write from. You don’t need to answer every one; you do need to connect, not summarize, and to point to your files by number.

  • What thread runs through more than one of your artifacts?
  • What did your 10 Things revisit show about how your thinking changed?
  • Where did your own results surprise you, or contradict something you believed in week 1?
  • What didn’t change — and should it have?
  • Module 6: how did looking at governance and the future change how you see your own choices?
  • What do you still question?

Caution

Missing a file? Leave the gap. Don’t renumber to close it, and don’t add a blank file. If you finish something late, it goes in under its own number.

Zip the folder

  • Windows: right-click the CSC141-portfolio folder and choose Compress to ZIP file (or Send to → Compressed (zipped) folder).
  • Mac: Control-click the folder and choose Compress.
  • Chromebook: open the folder in Files, select every file, right-click and choose Zip selection.
  • Google Drive: right-click the folder and choose Download — Drive zips it for you.

Rename the zip M6-Q03-[LastName]-Portfolio.zip and upload it to the M6-Q03 assignment in Canvas.
