Where Module 1 ends and Module 2 starts

The one idea to carry across

Everything in Module 1 was AI that sorts: spam or not spam, this topic or that one, the tagging in the dataset you took apart. Module 2 is AI that makes. Same field, genuinely different machine.

The connection worth carrying across: the bias you found in those 50 headlines doesn’t go away when a system starts generating instead of sorting. It gets harder to see, because a generated paragraph doesn’t show you what it left out.

What Monday, September 14 will be

We’ll open with a short wrap-up of the Module 1 discussion — short, because most of the session is new material. Then we start week 5.

Come with one concrete thing from either activity: a place where a tool’s description of itself didn’t match what you’d read, or a detail your sentence carried that a picture didn’t. That’s all — nothing written, nothing submitted.
