About this checklist

This is the running list of everything that should be in your CSC141-portfolio folder. It grows as the course does — each time a new artifact is assigned, it appears here with its number and its filename. Come back whenever you want to know where you stand.

Open your portfolio folder next to this page, tick off what's actually in there, and the summary below tells you what's missing. If you're not sure what any of this means yet, start with What your portfolio is.

Caution

This page doesn't remember your ticks. Nothing you do here is saved, sent anywhere, or seen by your instructor — close the tab and it resets. That's on purpose: the list is what matters, not the checkboxes. If you want a record of where you stood on a given day, use Save a status record at the bottom.
